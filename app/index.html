Connect Spotify

Spotify asks for a one-time setup before another app can play your music. It takes about two minutes. You need Spotify Premium.

  1. Open Spotify for Developers

    Log in with the Spotify account you listen on. Accept the terms if it asks.

  2. Create an app

    Tap Create app and fill it in like this. Tap a line to copy it.

    Under “Which API/SDKs are you planning to use?”, tick iOS and Web API.

    Tick the terms box and tap Save.

  3. Paste your Client ID

    On your new app’s page, copy the Client ID and paste it here.

  4. Connect

    Spotify opens and asks if Otocloud can play your music. Tap Agree to come back here.
