Privacy Policy

Otocloud · operated by UNIWA LLC · Last updated: November 19, 2026

Otocloud is an app for listening to music together with friends, with optional voice calls. This policy explains what we collect and why.

Otocloudは、友達と一緒に音楽を聴き、必要なら通話もできるアプリです。ここでは、集める情報とその理由を説明します。

What we collect

  • Account: your Otocloud handle and display name, and your profile picture if you set one.
  • SoundCloud (if you connect it): your SoundCloud user ID, username and avatar, and an access token so Otocloud can show your likes and playlists and like tracks on your behalf. We never see your SoundCloud password.
  • Friends and activity: who you follow in Otocloud, your online status, the track you are playing (you can turn this off in Settings), songs you pin as your mood (removed after 24 hours) and songs you send to friends.
  • Calls: voice is sent in real time through our calling provider (LiveKit) and is not recorded or stored by Otocloud. Music is never sent through calls; each person plays it from SoundCloud on their own device.
  • Device: basic technical data needed to run the service (such as error logs). We do not use advertising trackers.

集めるもの：ID・表示名・プロフィール画像、連携した場合のSoundCloudのID・名前・画像とログイン用の鍵（パスワードは見ません）、フォロー・オンライン状態・再生中の曲（設定でオフにできます）・気分の曲（24時間で消えます）・送った曲。通話の声は録音・保存しません。音楽は通話に乗せず、各自のiPhoneがSoundCloudから再生します。広告用の追跡はしません。

How we use it

Only to run Otocloud: showing your profile and music to your friends, syncing playback when you listen together, connecting calls, and keeping the service safe. We do not sell your data.

使い道はOtocloudを動かすためだけです。データを売ることはありません。

Who can see what

People who follow you can see your profile, online status, now playing (if on), your mood song, and songs you send them. Your SoundCloud token is never shown to anyone.

Services we use

  • SoundCloud (music and your linked account)
  • Google Firebase (account and database)
  • Cloudflare (server)
  • LiveKit (voice calls)

Deleting your data

You can disconnect SoundCloud at any time from Settings, and you can ask us to delete your account and data by contacting us. We delete it within 30 days.

SoundCloudの連携は設定からいつでも外せます。アカウントとデータの削除は、下の連絡先にご連絡ください。30日以内に削除します。

Children

Otocloud is not intended for children under 13.

Contact
